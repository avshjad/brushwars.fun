HOW IT WORKS

Buy brushes with MON. Each brush earns 1 pixel per minute (the "drip") until the round ends. Normal brushes paint empty tiles; golden brushes can overpaint anywhere.

Each round has two prizes: the NFT of the finished canvas goes to whoever places the last pixel, and the pot (30% of all brush sales) goes to whoever places the most pixels. The pot winner takes 90%; 10% rolls into the next round.

Trust model (read this): results — who painted, who won — are settled by an operator that we run. The smart contract holds and pays out all funds; the operator cannot withdraw or take your money, only report game outcomes. This is a trusted-operator design, not a fully trustless one. Pots are kept small while the project is young.

The finished artwork and its attributes are stored fully on-chain. Brush purchases, the pot, and prize payouts are all on-chain and verifiable.
